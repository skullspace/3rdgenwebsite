Skullspace

We are a hackerspace located on the 2nd floor of 374 Donald St. in Winnipeg.

We have open house nights on Tuesdays 6pm-8pm.

Our member interests span a broad range of playful tinkering.

Some features of our space include:

  • Classroom/dance floor with AV capabilities
  • Lounge
  • 3D printers and other, small scale computer driven prototype tools
  • Hand tools and electronics tools
  • Library
  • Kitchen
  • Lounge
  • Video games
  • Board games

As a member-run, all-volunteer, non-profit organization and community our members mostly use the space to work on their own projects. You may find someone who takes interest in your project, but results may vary. We do not offer commercial services. Many of our members have found the space useful to build friendships and to network, but this takes time.

Presentations

We rarely offer formal programming, but folks are always welcome to inquire about offering presentation of interest to our broad hackerspace community. We are life-long learners.

Once a year, typically in the spring/early summer we have an entire weekend of public festivities that include presentations. This is a great chance to practice your public speaking for a small audiance and have an automatic audience from folks attending for other reasons.
